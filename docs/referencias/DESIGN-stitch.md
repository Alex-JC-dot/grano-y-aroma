## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the sensory warmth, historical depth, and refined craft of specialty coffee culture anchored in the colonial heritage of Sucre, Bolivia. The visual identity bridges artisanal agricultural traceability (Yungas, Caranavi, Tarija) with a high-end specialty auction and retail experience.

The aesthetic blends **Colonial Heritage Elegance** and **Warm Tactile Minimalism**. Visual compositions draw from classic Andean and colonial archways, warm whitewashed adobe walls, terracotta roof tiles, and the rich sensory depth of roasted coffee beans. Interfaces avoid cold, clinical e-commerce tropes in favor of an inviting, editorial tactile space. Micro-elements echo artisanal wax seals, botanical coffee illustrations, and geographic certification stamps, instilling confidence among international auction bidders and local coffee lovers.

## Colors
The color hierarchy translates coffee cultivation, bean roasting, and Sucre’s whitewashed and terracotta architectural palette into digital surfaces:

- **Primary (`#3E2314` - Café Profundo):** Expresses the dense richness of dark roasted specialty beans. Used for prominent headers, primary navigational elements, structural borders, and high-emphasis typographic moments.
- **Secondary (`#843C0C` - Tostado Andino):** Evokes mid-roast complexity and warm clay masonry. Anchors secondary interactive controls, category tags, interactive states, and hover transitions.
- **Tertiary (`#C8902E` - Ámbar Dorado):** Directs auction actions, lot certifications, live bidding badges, and critical calls-to-action. Imparts warmth, prestige, and high perceived value without breaking culinary elegance.
- **Background & Canvas:**
  - Base canvas: `#FDF3EA` (Crema Sucre), providing a warm, unbleached parchment reading environment that reduces eye fatigue.
  - Surface muted: `#FCE4D6` (Durazno Suave), used for card containers, elevation tiering, and traceability metadata groupings.
- **Neutrals & Body Typography:**
  - Text primary: `#2B1E17` (neutral base), ensuring WCAG AAA legibility against cream backgrounds while avoiding the harshness of pure black.
  - Text secondary: `#6A5246` for timestamps, SCA cupping scores, and lot numbers.
  - Subtle borders: `#E8D2C2` for hairline dividers and container outlines.

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the stately, literary character of **Playfair Display** with the functional clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (Playfair Display):** Conveys heritage, colonial literature, and master craftsmanship. Used for auction headliners, farm origin stories, and major modal headings. Italic variants are reserved for sensory notes (e.g., *notas a jazmín, miel silvestre y bergamota*) and cupping provenance.
- **Body, Interface, & Numbers (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Balances geometric structure with friendly, open counters. Its neutral legibility ensures micro-data—such as altitude measurements (e.g., *1.850 m.s.n.m.*), SCA scores, auction bid logs, and logistics tracking—remains effortless to scan at all sizes.
- **Data & Cupping Labels:** Labels and lot chips utilize slight letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) in small capitals or medium weights to establish order without visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a balanced, generous spacing model that gives editorial imagery and auction charts breathing room:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** A 12-column grid with `margin: 2.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container width is capped at `1280px` for optimal reading flow and card balance. Multi-column auction boards reflow into dedicated live bid tables alongside detailed lot profiles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** An 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Complex auction sidebars collapse into sticky drawer sheets.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** A 4-column grid with `margin-sm: 1.25rem` and `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Cards and sensory wheels collapse to full-bleed carousels with horizontal swipe affordances.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Story-driven sections utilize `space-2xl` (4rem) to maintain an unhurried, artisanal rhythm between origin features, producer profiles, and auction lots.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics tangible, physical paper and polished wood surfaces rather than synthetic digital planes:

- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Drop shadows avoid cool blacks or grays. Shadows are tinted with deep roasted umber: `rgba(62, 35, 20, 0.08)` for base elevation and `rgba(62, 35, 20, 0.16)` for high-emphasis modals and active auction popovers.
- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#FDF3EA`. Surface cards rise using `#FFFFFF` with warm hairline borders (`#E8D2C2`, 1px solid), or nest within `#FCE4D6` to represent inset provenance panels and cupping scorecards.
- **Interactive Depth:** Floating auction timers and sticky bid strips use an offset blur of `0 8px 24px rgba(62, 35, 20, 0.12)`, grounding them gently against the parchment background.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides organic, approachable boundaries that echo the curvature of coffee beans and colonial Sucre architectural arches without feeling cartoonish:

- Base elements (buttons, text inputs, auction badges) utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- Product cards, interactive traceability passports, and auction bid containers utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Verification stamps, producer avatars, and cupping score indicators use full pill or circular geometry (`rounded-full`) to simulate wax stamps and quality seals.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary CTAs (Live Bids / Purchase):** Background in `#C8902E` (Ámbar Dorado) with text in `#2B1E17`, transition to `#843C0C` on hover with text in `#FDF3EA`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Border radius: `0.5rem`.
- **Secondary Actions (Story, Traceability Details):** Outlined with 1.5px solid `#3E2314`, transparent background, text in `#3E2314`. Hover fills with `#FCE4D6`.
- **Ghost Actions:** Text in `#843C0C`, no background, underline on focus/hover.

### Badges, Stamps & Chips
- **Cupping Score & SCA Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) badges. Background in `#FCE4D6` with a delicate border of `1px solid #C8902E`. Typographic style: `label-md` in bold `#3E2314`.
- **Traceability Stamp:** Distinct circular or scalloped emblem featuring producer name, department (e.g., "La Paz - Caranavi"), altitude, and harvest year.
- **Auction State Indicators:** 
  - Live: `#843C0C` background, pulsating `#C8902E` ping dot, white text.
  - Closed/Awarded: Subtle `#E8D2C2` fill with `#6A5246` text.

### Cards & Auction Containers
- **Product & Lot Cards:** Built on `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border of `#E8D2C2` and `rounded-xl` geometry. Images feature warm duotone lighting or natural estate photography. Lower container sections house lot specs (process: Lavado/Natural/Geisha, cupping score, current bid).
- **Interactive Bidding Panel:** Pinned or highlighted in `#FCE4D6` with a subtle inner glow. Features an incremental bidding step counter, dynamic currency toggle (BOB / USD), and instant confirmation status.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Input Fields:** Warm white `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #E8D2C2` border. In focus: border switches to `1.5px solid #843C0C` with a warm glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(132, 60, 12, 0.15)`). Placeholder text in `#8C776B`.
- **Selectors & Dropdowns:** Elegant minimal chevron icons styled in `#3E2314`.

### Lists & Traceability Timeline
- **Provenance Timeline:** Vertical timeline tracking the coffee journey from cherry harvest, wet milling, patio drying, dry milling, to Sucre roasting. Nodes represented by amber seed icons connected by a warm dashed rule (`#C8902E`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Selection Controls:** Custom circular and rounded-square controls bordered with `#843C0C`. When active, filled with `#3E2314` featuring an amber checkmark (`#C8902E`).
